## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, architectural approach to garment and textile care in Ghana. Balancing high-touch bespoke service with institutional corporate reliability, the brand language moves away from stereotypical bubble-laden, domestic cleaning tropes toward deliberate editorial minimalism, crisp tailoring metaphors, and structural poise.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Discerning Urban Professionals & Households:** Users seeking seamless, trustworthy garment preservation where high-value traditional attire (such as Kente, delicate linens, and formal wear) is handled with consummate precision.
- **Enterprise & Hospitality Clients:** Commercial partners seeking dependable turnaround, transparent logistics, and institutional standard compliance.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, crisp hygiene, seamless punctuality, and understated prestige.

### Aesthetic Foundation
The visual direction unites **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism** with functional **Corporate Modernism**. It prioritises:
- Generous, luminous breathing room anchored by a pristine warm-white canvas.
- Deep, assertive navy typography conveying legal-grade credibility and craftsmanship.
- Restrained brand blue accents derived strictly from the core emblem, paired with pale wash tints.
- Physical garment textures and purposeful photography crops over decorative gradients, glass filters, or non-functional embellishments.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate atmosphere of freshly laundered linen, structured water, and maritime depth. Chromatic selections strictly reflect the visual brand identity.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary Blue (`#0B80CA`):** The primary driver of customer agency. Reserved for core conversion triggers ("Book a Pickup"), highlighted navigation states, active step meters, and dynamic focal elements.
- **Secondary Deep Navy (`#102B51`):** The institutional anchor. Used for primary editorial headings, essential interface labels, structured containers, and high-emphasis visual weight. It delivers authoritative contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Tertiary Surface Blue (`#EDF7FC`):** The signature pale wash. Used to isolate service cards, information callouts, step guides, and active radio pills.
- **Neutral Slate (`#526477`):** Calibrated for secondary and supporting body copy, metadata timestamps, input borders, and assistive guidance.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFBFC`) & Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** The layered warm-white background system. Prevents screen glare on high-nit mobile displays while preserving immaculate editorial crispness.
- **Border Subtle (`#D9E7EF`):** A hairline boundary token for crisp card profiles, row dividing rules, and table segmentations without visual friction.
- **WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to the direct WhatsApp chat channel, ensuring instant recognition.

## Typography

The design system employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric construction and clean humanist stroke terminals reflect technical proficiency and refined customer hospitality.

### Typesetting Principles
- **Editorial Proportions:** Hero and page headings use aggressive negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`–`800`) to evoke publication-grade confidence.
- **Readable Running Copy:** Body copy uses comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) and uncompressed tracking to maintain legibility on handheld screens under direct outdoor Ghanaian daylight.
- **Action & Eyebrow Styling:** Metadata, operational step tags, and category chips employ uppercase or semi-bold micro-tracking (`label-small`) for immediate visual categorization.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows a disciplined 4px base coordinate scale tailored for an iPhone 16 Pro Max viewport (~440px wide) while adapting upwards to desktop viewports.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile Canvas:** 1-column fluid stacking with `20px` (`1.25rem`) side page gutters and `24px` content margins. Touch interaction zones observe a minimum 48px boundary.
- **Section Rhythm:** Major narrative transitions deploy `48px` to `72px` (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) vertical gaps, ensuring services, process walk-throughs, and branch directories feel distinct and unhurried.
- **Component Padding:** Standard card interior padding adheres to `1.25rem`–`1.5rem`, allowing deep navy headings and supporting text room to breathe against image containers.
- **Desktop Adaptation:** Translates to a 12-column layout maxing out at `1200px` content width with `24px` gutters and variable centering margins.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, premium garment care aesthetic, the design system avoids heavy drop shadows, plastic neomorphism, and ornate glass frosted blurs. Elevation is conveyed strictly through **tonal stratification** and **crisp hairline containment**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Canvas (Base Layer - `#FAFBFC`):** The structural floor of the mobile viewport.
2. **Surface Containers (Mid Layer - `#FFFFFF`):** Card structures, interactive list items, and form sheets raised subtly through pure white contrast and framed with a `1px solid #D9E7EF` hairline border.
3. **Tinted Accents (Embedded Layer - `#EDF7FC`):** Inset containers used for secondary callouts, pickup summaries, and branch hours, requiring no border or drop shadow.
4. **Floating Action Layer:** Persistent navigation bars and bottom action trays use an ultra-diffused ambient lift: `0px 4px 20px rgba(16, 43, 81, 0.06)` combined with a sharp bottom or top divider (`1px solid #D9E7EF`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced, contemporary rounded corner philosophy (`level 2`), communicating friendly approachability without softening into childlike or bubbly forms.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Buttons, Form Inputs & Chips (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Structured and intentional, maintaining crisp alignments along linear grid boundaries.
- **Cards, Content Modules & Visual Containers (`1rem` / `16px`):** Softens the visual density of multi-line service packages, branch location summaries, and gallery frames.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Trays (`1.5rem` / `24px` top corners):** Distinct, welcoming sheet presentations for pickup scheduling and branch contact sheets.
- **Pills / Status Dots (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for small status indicators (e.g., "Active", "Open Today", "Pending Confirmation").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Book a Pickup"):** Background `#0B80CA`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, height `52px`. Text weight `600`, centered with a subtle leading or trailing arrow icon. Hover/Active: `#0869A6`.
- **Secondary ("Visit a Branch"):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #102B51`, text `#102B51`, radius `8px`, height `52px`. Hover: `#EDF7FC`.
- **Chat Action ("Chat on WhatsApp"):** Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, height `48px`. Integrates the official WhatsApp glyph.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** Flat text in `#0B80CA` with an underlined hover state or chevron link indicator.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Height `52px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D9E7EF`, text `#102B51`, placeholder `#526477`, border radius `8px`, horizontal padding `16px`.
- **Focus State:** Border `1.5px solid #0B80CA`, ring `3px rgba(11, 128, 202, 0.15)`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Error State:** Border `1.5px solid #D32F2F`, error text `12px` below field.

### Service & Location Cards
- **Service Offering Cards:** Constructed with a `#FFFFFF` body, `1px solid #D9E7EF` perimeter, `16px` radius, and purposeful 16:10 aspect ratio photography at the top. Headings in `#102B51` followed by concise benefit points in `#526477`.
- **Branch Directory Cards:** Inset in `#EDF7FC` or framed `#FFFFFF`, featuring a live "Open / Closed" badge, direct navigation link, operating hours matrix, and direct branch dial trigger.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, padding `0 16px`, radius `8px`. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #D9E7EF` and `#526477` label. Active: `#102B51` background with `#FFFFFF` label.

### Form Checkboxes & Radio Selection Controls
- **Pickup Option Selectors:** Custom radio card groups wrapped in `1px solid #D9E7EF`. Selection triggers a transition to `#EDF7FC` background and `#0B80CA` active outline with a checkmark badge.

### Mobile Navigation Header & Floating Bar
- **Header:** Sticky `64px` height, surface `#FFFFFF`, border-bottom `1px solid #D9E7EF`. Features the authentic brand logo aligned left (unaltered, preserving native proportions) and a clean hamburger drawer trigger on the right.
- **Fixed Conversion Tray:** Compact sticky bottom bar displaying the dual conversion triggers: "Book Pickup" and "Find Branch" side-by-side without covering ongoing form interactions.
